<p>Docspress has a deliberately small content contract. The editor makes the meaningful decisions; the theme makes those decisions readable.</p>

<div class="section-summary"><strong>Portable by design.</strong> If Docspress is deactivated, the Pages and Posts remain standard WordPress content. Only the presentation layer changes.</div>
