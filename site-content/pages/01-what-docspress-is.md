<h2>A document surface for WordPress</h2>
<p>Docspress treats a documentation site as a reading environment, not as a collection of application screens. The left tree answers “where am I?”, the article answers “what does this say?”, and the page TOC answers “what is inside this page?”.</p>

<h2>The core rule</h2>
<p>A selected Docs Root Page is the beginning of the tree. Its published descendants are sorted by Page order and then title. This keeps the navigation explainable to editors and predictable to readers.</p>

<h3>What stays ordinary</h3>
<p>Posts remain Posts, media remains media, and the Classic Theme does not claim ownership of the site's content. The design system is a lens over WordPress rather than a replacement for it.</p>
