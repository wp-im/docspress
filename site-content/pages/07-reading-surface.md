<h2>The article rail</h2>
<p>On wide screens, headings can occupy a left editorial rail while paragraphs remain in a comfortable reading column. This gives sections a visual address without turning the document into a card grid.</p>

<h2>Margin material</h2>
<p>Notes, captions, and short definitions can sit near the passage they explain. They remain optional: ordinary paragraphs still read well when no special block is present.</p>

<h2>One restrained accent</h2>
<p>Orange marks links, section rules, and active states. It is a wayfinding signal rather than a decorative theme applied to every surface.</p>
