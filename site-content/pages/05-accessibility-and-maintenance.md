<p>Accessibility and maintenance are not postscript concerns. They define whether the theme can be used, trusted, and updated after the first attractive screenshot.</p>

<div class="docspress-callout"><strong>Operational rule</strong><span>Every live change should have a known target, a verified package, and a previous state that can be restored.</span></div>
