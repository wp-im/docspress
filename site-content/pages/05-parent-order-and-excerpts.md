<h2>Parent gives a page its place</h2>
<p>Use the Page Attributes panel to select the parent. Do not reproduce the tree in a menu: the Docs Tree is generated from the Page relationships.</p>

<h2>Order gives a page its sequence</h2>
<p>Use menu order in increments such as 10, 20, and 30. When two Pages share an order, Docspress uses their titles as a deterministic tie-breaker.</p>

<h2>Excerpt gives a page its first sentence</h2>
<p>The excerpt becomes the deck on an article and the description in a section directory. Write it as a compact promise rather than a duplicated first paragraph.</p>
