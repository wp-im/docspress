<h2>Wide screens keep the map visible</h2>
<p>The Docs Tree stays in a persistent left rail. The optional page TOC stays on the right. The article occupies the space between them.</p>

<h2>Tablet keeps the article grammar</h2>
<p>As the viewport narrows, the global and local navigation simplify before the document itself is compressed. The article rail remains long enough to preserve its editorial relationship.</p>

<h2>Phone prioritizes one task</h2>
<p>The chapter tree, page TOC, and global menu become separate drawers. Each has an independent opener, close control, Escape behavior, and focus return path.</p>
