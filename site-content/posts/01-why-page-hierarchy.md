---

<p>A documentation tree does not need to begin with a custom database model. WordPress Pages already have parents, ordering, titles, excerpts, revisions, permissions, and familiar editing screens.</p>

<p>Docspress builds on that existing grammar. The result is less spectacular in the database and more useful in daily editorial work: an editor can move a Page, change its order, and immediately understand what happens to the reading path.</p>

<p>This is also a portability decision. If the theme changes, the documents remain Pages. Their relationships remain inspectable. The site does not lose its content because a presentation layer was replaced.</p>
